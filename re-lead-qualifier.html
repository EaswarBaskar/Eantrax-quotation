🎯 What to Look For
❌
No Website — Best leads. They need you most. Check if GMB has only a phone number.
⚠️
Bad Website — Broken links, no SSL (http://), ugly design, not mobile-friendly.
🕒
Outdated — Copyright says 2015–2020, listings not updated, old phone/address.
📍
Speed tip — Open 10 GMB tabs at once, scan each for website link. No link = instant lead.
⭐
Bonus — Agents with 4+ stars but bad web presence = highly motivated prospects.
📞
Reach out — Mention you found them on Google Maps and noticed their web presence needs work. Very specific = higher reply rate.
